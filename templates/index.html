Clinical Impact of Classification Discordance

When independent diagnostic laboratories analyze the exact same genetic mutation, discordant interpretations (e.g., laboratory A asserting Pathogenic while laboratory B reports Benign or VUS) delay vital clinical therapies and confuse genetic counseling. MutationHunter discovers the exact biomarker signatures that resolve this uncertainty automatically.

Genetic Algorithm (GA) Optimization Framework
Darwinian Search

Simulates natural selection across 30 candidate features to maximize discriminatory ROC-AUC while penalizing redundant biomarker dimensionality.

Chromosome
Binary vector $\mathbf{c} \in \{0,1\}^{30}$, where bit $i=1$ includes feature $i$.
Fitness Function
$\text{Fitness} = 0.92 \cdot \text{AUC}_{\text{CV}} + 0.08 \cdot \left(1 - \frac{k}{30}\right)$
Crossover & Mutation
Uniform Crossover ($P_c = 0.85$) + Bit-Flip Mutation ($P_m = 0.04$).
Elitist Selection
Tournament Selection ($k=3$) with top-2 elite chromosomes preserved.
